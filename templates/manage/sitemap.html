Submit to Google

  1. Open Google Search Console and sign in with the business Google account.
  2. Add property → Domain → {{ request.get_host }}, and verify it with the DNS record it gives (add it in Namecheap → Domain → Advanced DNS).
  3. Go to Sitemaps, enter sitemap.xml and press Submit.

Submit to Bing (also feeds ChatGPT search)

  1. Open Bing Webmaster Tools.
  2. Choose Import from Google Search Console (fastest), or add the site by hand.
  3. Under Sitemaps, submit {{ site_url }}/sitemap.xml.
Also worth doing: a free Google Business Profile with the same name, address and phone as Settings. It drives "car rental near me" results on Google Maps and in AI answers.
